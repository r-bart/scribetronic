# Writing style — Roberto

The voice every post inherits. Format-specific skills (devlog, hot-take, etc.) override structure and length, never voice.

## 1. Voice & tone

Direct, confident, honest. The reader should feel they're hearing from someone who's actually done the thing — not someone teaching from a book.

Tensions to maintain:
- **Authoritative but not preachy.** State the rule. Don't lecture about why it matters for three paragraphs.
- **Personal but not confessional.** Use "I" freely. Don't dump emotion.
- **Educational but not flexing.** Numbers are evidence, not trophies. (Anti-MRR-flex stance — explicit in MW#3.)
- **Pragmatic over inspirational.** "If it doesn't work, kill it" beats "embrace the journey" every time.

**Positive example (own):** _"I haven't become a millionaire (yet), but I've done the hardest part—earning my first $75k."_ — concrete, honest, slight wink, no humble-brag.

**Negative example (own — from MW#1 closer):** _"Embarking on this journey to build 100 projects is not just about reaching a numerical goal, but about the continuous learning, growth, and innovation that comes with it."_ — abstract, three-noun stack, hollow, AI-shaped.

## 2. Structure

- **TL;DR or thesis up front.** First 2 sentences must give the reader the point. If you can cut everything after the third paragraph and still keep the message, do it.
- **Descriptive H2s, not clever ones.** "What is MakerOps?" beats "Meet the new kid on the block."
- **Short paragraphs.** 1-3 sentences. Single-sentence paragraphs are punctuation.
- **Bullets only when enumerating.** Never to pad. Three bullets minimum or it's a list of 1-2 disguised as a list.
- **Closers must close, not soften.** Specific next action, declarative statement, or one-line punch. Never "let's see where it takes us."

## 3. Sentence-level

- **Length variance is the engine.** 4-12-20-6. Don't write three medium sentences in a row.
- **Em-dashes over parentheses for asides.** Cleaner ritmo. Reserve `(parens)` for genuine side-info that could be cut.
- **Cut hedges.** "I think", "probably", "kind of", "really", "truly", "honestly". One per post max — and only if it carries weight.
- **Strong verbs, plain nouns.** "Ship" not "deliver". "Tool" not "solution". "Built" not "created".
- **Spanish→English leak watch.** Avoid: "specially" (use "especially"), "actually" as filler (use "in fact" or cut), Latinisms. Keep English idiomatic.

## 4. Signature moves (keep these)

1. **Number up front.** _"Total Sales: €0"_, _"two out of about 25 products"_, _"$75k"_. Lead with the real figure. It's your single biggest differentiator.
2. **The honest aside.** A short line that admits the unflattering truth before the reader can object. _"I'm not yet seen as an authority in the segment I'm targeting."_
3. **Rule + counter-rule pair.** State the conventional advice, then your version. (Borrowed from Koe, but used sparingly.)
4. **Welsh-style punctuation paragraph.** A 2-3 word sentence on its own line. _"Why?"_ _"That's it."_ Limit: ONE per post. More than that = tic.
5. **Concrete artifact reference.** Name the tool, the URL, the dollar amount, the date. Vague references signal you don't actually do the thing.

## 5. Anti-patterns / blacklist

| Pattern | Why kill it | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| "not just X, but Y" | AI signature; vague | Drop the negation. Say what it IS. |
| Three-adjective stack ("ambitious, comprehensive, deep") | Padding | Pick the one adjective that earns its place. |
| Soft closer ("Let's see where it takes us", "I invite you to follow along") | Coward's exit | Replace with: specific next step, hard punch, or just "—Roberto." |
| Vague future tense ("I'll naturally promote…") | Empty promise | Replace with: dated commitment or cut. |
| TL;DR that isn't a TL;DR | Mislabels content | A real TL;DR compresses the ARGUMENT in 1 sentence, not the structure. |
| "Specially" / "actually" filler | Spanish leak | "especially" / cut. |
| Coach-speak ("transformational", "journey", "embark", "unleash") | Sounds like LinkedIn 2017 | Use plain verbs. "Build", "ship", "learn", "fail". |
| Multiple rhetorical questions in a row | Welsh tic when overused | One per post max. Earn it. |
| Adverb stack ("incredibly powerful", "truly remarkable") | Lazy intensifiers | Cut the adverb. If the noun is weak, fix the noun. |

## 6. Positive examples (from own + references)

**Number-led opener (own, MW#2):** _"Total Sales: €0"_ — sets stakes, kills suspense, builds trust.

**Welsh punctuation rhythm:** _"Simple? Yes. Difficult to achieve? Absolutely!"_ — Roberto already does this naturally. Keep, but cap at once per post.

**Röhl-style honest aside:** _"Not the flashiest release, but exactly the kind of work that keeps the app solid for the long run."_ — admit the unsexy truth, then frame it.

**Welsh closer:** _"Just start. Your future self will thank you."_ — declarative, command, no equivocation.

**Moretti tag-line:** _"The conversation is the work."_ — coin a phrase that compresses the thesis. Aspirational target.

## 7. Negative examples (cut these on sight)

**From MW#1:** _"Embarking on this journey to build 100 projects is not just about reaching a numerical goal, but about the continuous learning, growth, and innovation that comes with it. It's about finding solutions, creating value, and making a real impact."_

What's wrong: "not just X, but Y" + three-noun stack + abstract nouns + zero specifics. Reader gets nothing concrete.

Fix: _"100 projects. The number isn't the point — the rate of learning is. I'll trade five mediocre launches for one that teaches me something I couldn't have read in a book."_

**From MW#3:** _"This community is designed for those truly committed to achieving significant growth and impact in their solopreneurial endeavors."_

What's wrong: "truly", "significant", "endeavors", abstraction. Sounds like a SaaS landing page.

Fix: _"The community is for people building seriously, not casually. If you ship once a quarter, this isn't for you."_

## 8. Revision checklist

Run before publishing any post:

- [ ] First 2 sentences contain the point.
- [ ] Closer is declarative, not soft. (No "Let's see…")
- [ ] At most ONE rhetorical question.
- [ ] At most ONE Welsh-punctuation paragraph ("That's it." / "Why?").
- [ ] Zero "not just X, but Y" constructions.
- [ ] Zero adjective stacks of 3+.
- [ ] At least one concrete number, name, or date.
- [ ] No filler hedges (kind of, really, truly, honestly, probably) unless load-bearing.
- [ ] No coach-speak (journey, embark, transformational, unleash).
- [ ] Sentence length varies — read aloud test.
- [ ] If "I" appears in a feeling-claim, it's earned by a fact next to it.

If any check fails, revise. If 3+ fail, the draft isn't ready.
